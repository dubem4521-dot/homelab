## What I Learned

- **Projects do not die from neglect.** They die from being abandoned. A five day gap is fine. A five month gap is not.
- **Coming back after a break requires re loading context.** The first 30 minutes of the next session were spent re reading my own code. That is normal and expected.
- **Time boxing helps here.** Knowing I have 4 months total means a 5 day gap is 4 percent of the budget. Not fatal, but not free either.
- **Writing the plan down matters when you come back.** Because the roadmap was documented, I did not have to re plan. Just resume.

---

## If This Happens Again

When you have to step away from a project:

1. **Leave a note for future you.** A comment in the code, a TODO list, a commit message. Something that says "resume here."
2. **Commit and push before stepping away.** Never leave uncommitted work when you are about to take a break. You will forget.
3. **Do not feel guilty.** Gaps are normal. The plan accounts for them.
4. **When you come back, spend the first 30 minutes reading, not coding.** Refresh context. Then work.
